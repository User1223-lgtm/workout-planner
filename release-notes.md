# Release Notes

<p class="lead">What's new in each version of Workout Planner. Your version is shown in <strong>Settings › Version</strong>.</p>

## Version 1.0.1 <span class="badge new">Coming soon</span>

- **Delete your workout history**: a new **Data** section in Settings with **Delete Workout History**. It empties the calendar and the statistics of the Me tab, and keeps your saved workouts. See [History & Statistics](history-and-statistics.html#delete-your-history).
- **A bigger timer**: the ring and the countdown now use all the free space of the screen, on every iPhone and iPad.

## Version 1.0 — November 2026

The first version of Workout Planner.

- **My Workouts**: folders and sub-folders like in the Files app, search, sort by name or date, rename, duplicate, move, multi-selection and delete.
- **8 templates**: Tabata, EMOM, AMRAP, HIIT 30/30, Circuit Training, Double Tabata, Strength Sets, Running Intervals.
- **Editor** <span class="badge pro">Pro</span>: exercises, rests and groups with rounds, nested at any depth.
- **AI workouts** made on your device by Apple Intelligence, from 7 questions.
- **Timer**: full-screen countdown ring, Get Ready, 3-2-1 beeps heard in silent mode and in the background, haptics, Live Activity with Pause and Next buttons.
- **Me**: calendar of completed workouts, weekly and monthly charts, averages, totals, and shareable stats images.
- **Widgets**: Quick Start and Stats, on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen.
- **Reminders** every morning and evening, and a monthly free AI workout reminder for Pro.
- **Workout Planner Pro** <span class="badge pro">Pro</span>: create from scratch, edit, import and export JSON files, 1 free AI workout per month.
