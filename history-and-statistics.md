## What is recorded

A workout is recorded when the timer reaches the **end** (or when you skip past the last step). For each completed workout, the app keeps:

| Data | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Workout name | A copy of the name at the time. The history stays even if you later rename or delete the workout. |
| Date and time | When it started and when it finished. |
| **Time** | The real time from start to finish, pauses included. |
| **Exercises** | The number of exercises of the workout (rounds included). |
| **Heart rate** | With an [Apple Watch](apple-watch.html): every measure of the workout, its average and its maximum. |
| **Calories** | Calculated from the heart rate and your profile. See [Heart rate and calories](#heart-rate-and-calories). |

A workout ended early with **✕ › End** is not recorded. Everything is stored only on your device, and in the Health app if you turn on [Apple Health](#apple-health).

The **Me** tab has four parts, selected at the top: **Workouts**, **Data**, **Goals** and **Profile**.

## Workouts: the calendar

{% include screen.html image="me-workouts.jpg" alt="The calendar of November with completed workouts" caption="Me › Workouts" class="right" %}

- The **month** is shown at the top. Use **‹** and **›** to go to the previous or next month.
- Under the month: the number of workouts and the total time of that month (*9 workouts · 8h 18m 55s*), or *No workout this month*.
- The **calendar** starts on the first day of the week of your region (Monday in France, Sunday in the United States).
- A **green dot** under a day means you completed at least one workout that day. **Today** is circled.
- **Tap a day** to see its workouts below the calendar, with the finish time, the **Time** and the **Exercises**. If you trained twice that day, both are listed. A day without workout says *No workout completed on this day.*

Workouts are placed on the day they **finished**.

### Workout details

**Tap a workout** under the calendar to open its details:

- its name, date and time;
- **Time** and **Exercises**, and **Calories** when they were calculated;
- **Heart Rate**, when it was measured: average, maximum and minimum BPM, and the **chart** of your heart rate over the whole workout;
- at the bottom, in small grey text, what was missing and how to get it next time (heart rate or calories). Nothing is shown when both are there;
- **Share** (top right): a dark image of the workout with its numbers and its heart rate chart, for Messages, Instagram, Save Image…

**Done** closes the details.

## Data: the statistics

{% include screen.html image="me-data.jpg" alt="Charts of the week and the month" caption="Me › Data" class="right" %}

Before your first completed workout, this page says **No Data Yet**.

### This Week and This Month

Two cards, each with **four charts**. Swipe a card sideways (or tap the dots under it) to switch between them:

| Chart | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Workouts** (green) | Number of completed workouts |
| **Training Time** (blue) | Real time spent training |
| **Exercises** (red) | Number of exercises done |
| **Calories** (pink) | Calories of the workouts where they were calculated |

- **This Week**: one bar per day of the current week. The current day is highlighted.
- **This Month**: one bar per week of the current month: *Week 1* = days 1–7, *Week 2* = 8–14, *Week 3* = 15–21, *Week 4* = from the 22nd to the end of the month.

The total of the period is shown at the top right of each chart.

### Averages

| Number | How it is calculated |
|---|---|
| **Workouts per Week** | Number of workouts ÷ number of weeks since the week of your first workout (this week included) |
| **Workouts per Month** | Number of workouts ÷ number of months since the month of your first workout (this month included) |
| **Average Duration** | Total real time ÷ number of workouts |
| **Average Exercises** | Total exercises ÷ number of workouts |
| **Average Heart Rate** | Average of the workouts where the heart rate was measured |
| **Average Calories** | Calories ÷ number of workouts where they were calculated |

### Totals

**Total Workouts**, **Total Training Time**, **Total Exercises** and **Total Calories**, since your first workout.

The heart rate and the calories only count the workouts where they were measured. When there is none yet, they show **-**.

## Goals: your targets

**Me › Goals** lists your goals as cards. A goal is a number to reach **every week, month or year**, or **between two dates** of your choice:

| Count | Example |
|---|---|
| **Workouts** | 3 workouts every week |
| **Time** | 8 hours of training every month |
| **Exercises** | 5,000 exercises every year |
| **Calories** | 1,500 kcal every week (calories are calculated with an [Apple Watch](apple-watch.html)) |
| Any of them, **Custom** | 20 workouts from December 1 to January 15 |

Each card shows the goal (*3 workouts*), its period in a small label at the top right (*This Week*, or *01/12/2026 – 15/01/2027* for custom dates), a **progress bar** and the progress (*2 / 3*). The progress keeps counting after the target: *5 / 3* if you did more than planned.

- **Completed**: as soon as the target is reached, the card turns **indigo**, with **Completed** before the numbers (*Completed · 5 / 3*). The layout doesn't change.
- **Failed**: when the dates of a **Custom** goal are over without reaching it, the card turns **grey**, with **Failed**.
- **Add a goal**: tap **+** at the top right (or **Add a Goal** when the list is empty), choose what to count, the period (**Week**, **Month**, **Year** or **Custom**) and the number, then tap **Add**. The number is chosen with **wheels**, to the unit: 1 to 999 workouts, 1 to 9,999 exercises, hours and minutes of training, or 10 to 99,990 kcal (by 10).
- **Custom** shows **From** and **To**: the goal counts the workouts completed between these two days, both included.
- **Delete a goal**: touch and hold its card › **Delete Goal**, then **Delete**. A goal can't be changed: to change one, delete it and add a new one.
- You can have as many goals as you want, for example a weekly one and a yearly one.

Goals don't record anything new: the progress is counted from your **completed workouts** (the same data as the calendar and the statistics), for the **current** week (Monday to Sunday or Sunday to Saturday, as set on your device), month or year, or between the custom dates. A weekly, monthly or yearly goal starts again at 0 at the start of each new period. If you delete workouts from the history, the progress goes down too.

The **Goals** widget shows the same progress bars on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. See [Widgets](widgets.html#goals).

Goals also send [reminders](notifications.html#goal-reminders) with the time left.

## Profile

**Me › Profile** keeps your characteristics, only on your device:

| Field | How to fill it in |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Your first name, typed on the right. The daily notifications and the voice coach use it. |
| **Sex** | Female, Male or Other |
| **Birthday** | A wheel opens when you tap the row |
| **Height** | Wheels, in cm (or feet and inches) |
| **Weight** | Wheels, to 0.1 kg (or in pounds) |
| **Units** | **Metric** or **Imperial** |

The **BMI** is shown when the height and the weight are filled in. To empty the profile: **Settings › Data › Delete Profile**.

## Heart rate and calories

**The heart rate** is recorded only when a workout is measured by an [Apple Watch](apple-watch.html) (Pro): a workout played on the Watch. Every measure is kept, for the chart of the workout. Workouts done on the iPhone or the iPad alone have no heart rate.

**The calories** are calculated by the app, once, at the end of the workout, from:

- every heart rate measure of the workout;
- your **sex**, your **age** (from your birthday) and your **weight**, from Me › Profile.

They are calculated **if and only if your profile is completely filled in** (name, sex, birthday, height and weight). Otherwise the workout has no calories. The formula is the one most heart rate monitors use (Keytel et al., 2005). The number is saved with the workout and doesn't change if you edit your profile later.

The Health app may show slightly different calories for the same workout: Apple calculates them its own way.

## Share your stats

When you have at least one workout, a **share** button appears at the top right of the **Data** page. Choose **Share My Stats**:

| Choice | Image |
|---|---|
| **This Week** | The charts of the week |
| **This Month** | The charts of the month |
| **All Time** | All the averages and totals |

The calories chart is included when there are calories in the period. To share one workout, use **Share** in its [details](#workout-details).

The app draws a dark image (1080 pixels wide, PNG named *My Workout Stats.png*) titled **My Training Stats**, with the app logo and the line *Plan and time your workouts with Workout Planner*, plus a link to the app on the App Store. Then the iOS share sheet opens: Messages, WhatsApp, Instagram, Save Image, AirDrop…

## Delete your history

**Settings › Data › Delete Workout History** deletes every completed workout: the calendar and all the statistics of the Me tab are emptied, and the Stats widget goes back to zero.

- Your **saved workouts are not touched**: only the history is deleted.
- The app asks for a confirmation first. **This can't be undone.**
- The button is greyed out when the history is already empty.

To delete **one** completed workout, select its day in the calendar, touch and hold its card, tap **Delete Entry**, then **Delete**.

When [Apple Health](#apple-health) is on, the deleted workouts are also deleted from the Health app.

## Apple Health

Turn on **Settings › Permissions › Apple Health** to save your completed workouts in Apple's **Health** app. The first time, iOS asks for the permission: allow **Workouts**.

Health then always matches your history:

| In Workout Planner | In the Health app |
|---|---|
| You complete a workout | It is added, with its start, its end and the name of the workout. A workout measured by the Apple Watch is saved by the Watch, with its heart rate and calories |
| You turn Apple Health on | The workouts already in your history are added too |
| You delete a workout from the history, or the whole history | It is deleted from Health too |
| You turn Apple Health off | Nothing new is added or deleted. The workouts already in Health stay there |

- The workouts appear in Health as **High Intensity Interval Training**, from the source *Workout Planner*. Their duration is the real time, pauses included.
- The app only changes the workouts saved by Workout Planner (on the iPhone or by its Apple Watch app), never those of other apps.
- A workout you delete yourself in the Health app is not added back.
- If the permission was refused, the option turns itself off. Open the **Health** app, tap your profile picture, then **Apps › Workout Planner**, and turn on **Workouts**.
- Apple Health is available on iPhone, and on iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. It is free (no Pro needed).
