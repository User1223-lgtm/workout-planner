## Start a workout

There are three ways to start a workout:

- Open it and tap **Start Workout**.
- In any list, **touch and hold** it and choose **Start Workout**.
- Tap it in the [Quick Start widget](widgets.html#quick-start) on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.
- On your [Apple Watch](apple-watch.html) (Pro): tap the workout, then **Start Workout**.

A workout that lasts 0 seconds can't be started (the button is greyed out).

## The timer screen

{% include screen.html image="timer.jpg" alt="The timer during the Heavy Bag exercise, in the first of ten rounds" caption="An exercise in progress" class="right" %}

From top to bottom:

1. **✕** to end the workout, the **workout name**, **Step 2 of 24**, and the **total time left** for the whole workout.
2. A **progress bar** for the whole workout.
3. The **rounds** you are in, when the workout has groups: one orange chip per group, from the outermost to the innermost (*Intervals 3/10*).
4. The **kind of step** (<span class="badge prepare">GET READY</span> <span class="badge exercise">EXERCISE</span> <span class="badge rest">REST</span>) and its **name**.
5. The **ring** and the **countdown** of the current step. The ring empties as time passes.
6. The **description** of the step (the coaching cue), if there is one.
7. **NEXT**: the next step and its duration, or **Finish** on the last step.
8. The **controls**.

The background color follows the current step: blue for Get Ready, red for exercises, green for rests, and green again when the workout is complete.

## Controls

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **⏸ / ▶** (center) | Pauses or resumes. While paused, **Paused** appears under the countdown. |
| **⏪** (left) | Restarts the current step. If the step started **less than 2 seconds ago**, goes back to the **previous** step instead. Tap twice quickly to go back one step. |
| **⏩** (right) | Skips to the next step. On the last step, it ends the workout (it counts as completed). |
| **✕** (top left) | Asks **End Workout?** — **End** closes the timer, **Cancel** keeps going. |

<blockquote class="warning" markdown="1">

A workout ended with **✕ › End** is **not** saved in your history. Only workouts that reach the end are saved.

</blockquote>

## The steps

- Every workout starts with a **Get Ready** countdown of **10 seconds**.
- Then each exercise and each rest is played in order, and groups are played round after round. See [How the timer reads your workout](creating-workouts.html#how-the-timer-reads-your-workout).
- The timer moves to the next step by itself when the countdown reaches zero.

## Sounds

The beeps are generated by the app and are designed to be heard during a workout:

| Moment | Sound |
|---|---|
| The last **3, 2, 1** seconds of every step | A short beep each second |
| An **exercise** starts | Two high beeps |
| A **rest** starts | Two descending beeps |
| **Halfway** through a step (steps of 10 seconds or more) | A short rising *ding-ding* |
| The workout is **complete** | Three rising beeps |

- Beeps are played **even when your iPhone is in silent mode**, at the **media volume** (use the volume buttons during the workout).
- They **mix with your music or podcast**: you don't need to stop it.
- They keep playing when the **screen is locked** or when you switch to another app.
- After a phone call or a Siri request, or when you connect or disconnect headphones, the sounds start again by themselves.
- To turn every beep off: **Settings › Sounds** (this also turns the voice coach off).

## Voice coach

A voice (in English) tells you what's happening, right after the beep:

| Moment | The voice says |
|---|---|
| Before the first step | *Get ready* |
| An exercise starts | Its name and duration: *Push-ups, 30 seconds* |
| A rest starts | *Rest, 15 seconds* |
| Halfway through an exercise of 20 seconds or more | *Halfway* |
| The workout is complete | *Workout complete. Great job!* — with your first name when it's filled in [Me › Profile](history-and-statistics.html#profile): *Great job, Thomas!* |

The 3-2-1 countdown stays in beeps (a voice would be late). The voice speaks over your music without stopping it; a podcast or an audiobook is paused while it speaks. Turn it off in **Settings › Voice Coach** (it needs **Sounds**).

## Haptics and screen

- Your iPhone gives a **strong tap** at every step change, and a **success** vibration at the end.
- The screen **stays on** during the workout, so you can watch the timer without touching the phone.

## With an Apple Watch

With [Workout Planner Pro](pro.html), a workout started on your iPhone locks the Workout Planner app on your Apple Watch, which shows *Workout on your iPhone or iPad* until the end. A workout started on the Watch locks the iPhone the same way (*Workout on Apple Watch*). To measure your heart rate, start the workout on the Watch. See [Apple Watch](apple-watch.html#one-workout-at-a-time).

## Lock Screen, Dynamic Island and background

While a workout is running, a **Live Activity** shows the timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with **Pause/Resume** and **Next** buttons. See [Widgets & Live Activity](widgets.html#live-activity).

The timer is based on the clock, not on counting seconds: if you leave the app or lock the phone, it stays exact, and when you come back it is exactly where it should be (it can even have moved through several steps).

## The end of the workout

When the last step ends, the **Workout Complete!** screen shows the workout name and its tiles:

- **Time**: the real time from start to finish, **pauses included**.
- **Exercises**: the number of exercise steps of the workout.

Tap **Done** to close it. The workout is saved in [Me › Workouts](history-and-statistics.html) with its time and exercises, and your statistics, goals and widgets are updated.

After your 1st, 5th and 15th completed workouts, Apple's rating window may appear over this screen to ask what you think of the app. Rating is optional: tap **Not Now** to close it. iOS decides whether it appears and shows it at most three times a year. You can rate the app at any time in **Settings › Rate on the App Store**.

## Good to know

- Only one workout can run at a time. Links from widgets are ignored while the timer is open.
- If the app is closed by force during a workout, the leftover Live Activity is removed the next time you open the app, and that workout is not saved.
- The ring and the countdown use all the free space of the screen, on iPhone and on iPad.
