## Add a widget

**Home Screen**

1. Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
2. Tap **Edit › Add Widget** (or **+** at the top left).
3. Search for **Workout Planner**, choose **Quick Start** or **Stats**, choose a size, then **Add Widget**.

**Lock Screen**

1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap **Customize › Lock Screen**.
2. Tap the widget area under the time, then choose **Workout Planner**.

**Choose what a widget shows**: touch and hold the widget, then **Edit Widget**.

## Quick Start

Start a workout in one tap.

| Size | Shows |
|---|---|
| Small | 1 workout |
| Medium | 3 workouts |
| Lock Screen (rectangular) | 1 workout |

**Tap a workout** in the widget: Workout Planner opens and the timer starts right away. (If a workout is already running, the tap only opens the app.)

### Choose the workouts

**Edit Widget › Workouts** lets you pick the workouts to show (1 for small and Lock Screen widgets, up to 3 for medium). Each one is listed with its folder and duration, for example *Strength › Week 1 · 25 min*.

If you leave the list **empty**, the widget shows **your last workouts**: the ones you started most recently, completed by your newest workouts. Workouts that last 0 seconds are never shown.

If you have no workout yet, the widget says *No workouts yet — Create one in the app*.

## Stats

Your progress at a glance.

| Size | Shows |
|---|---|
| Small | The number of the period (for example *3 workouts*), with a small chart under it |
| Medium | The chart, the same as in **Me › Data** |
| Lock Screen, circular | The number |
| Lock Screen, rectangular | The number and a small chart |

The widget switches to the new week or month by itself at midnight.

**Edit Widget › Statistic** offers six choices:

- **Workouts This Week** (default) · **Training Time This Week** · **Exercises This Week**
- **Workouts This Month** · **Training Time This Month** · **Exercises This Month**

Tap the widget to open the **Me** tab.

## Goals

The progress bars of your goals of **Me › Goals**, in the same order.

| Size | Shows |
|---|---|
| Small | The first 2 goals: period, progress (*2 / 3*) and bar |
| Medium | The first 3 goals, with what each one counts |
| Large | The first 6 goals |
| Lock Screen, rectangular | The first goal |

A reached goal shows a green check. The widget starts again at 0 by itself when a new week, month or year begins. With no goal yet, it says *Add a goal in Me › Goals*.

Tap the widget to open **Me › Goals**. See [Goals](history-and-statistics.html#goals-your-targets).

## When widgets update

Widgets are refreshed when you complete a workout, when you open the app, and when you leave it. If a widget looks out of date (for example after you renamed a workout), open Workout Planner once.

Workouts deleted in the app disappear from the widgets at the next refresh.

## Live Activity

While a workout is running, the timer also appears:

- on the **Lock Screen**, as a notification-like card;
- in the **Dynamic Island** on iPhones that have one (compact while you use another app, expanded when you touch and hold it).

It shows the kind of step and its color, the step name and the rounds (*EXERCISE · Circuit A 2/3*), the **countdown** of the step with a bar that empties, and *Step 5/24 · Ends at 18:42* (or the time left when paused).

Two buttons control the timer without opening the app:

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| **Pause / Resume** | Same as the center button of the timer |
| **Next** | Skips to the next step |

The countdown updates by itself, even when the app is in the background. The Live Activity ends when you close the timer.

<blockquote class="tip" markdown="1">

If you don't see the Live Activity, check **Settings › Workout Planner › Live Activities** in the Settings app of your iPhone.

</blockquote>
