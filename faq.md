## General

### Is Workout Planner free?
Yes. The app is free, with no ads. Templates, the timer, folders, history, statistics and widgets are free. [Workout Planner Pro](pro.html) adds creating from scratch, editing, import and export, and 1 free AI workout per month. AI workouts can also be bought one by one.

### Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up and no login. Purchases use your Apple Account.

### Does it work without internet?
Yes. Everything works offline, including AI workouts once Apple Intelligence is ready on your device. Only buying, managing and restoring purchases need the App Store.

### Which languages are available?
The app is in English.

### Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 26.5 or later, and Apple Watch with watchOS 26.5 or later (with [Pro](pro.html)). AI workouts need a device with Apple Intelligence.

### How can I rate the app or leave a review?
Open **Settings › Rate on the App Store**: the App Store opens on the review page of Workout Planner. Thank you, it really helps!

### Why does the app ask me to rate it?
After your 1st, 5th and 15th completed workouts, the app may show Apple's rating window on the **Workout Complete!** screen. iOS shows it at most three times a year, and you can close it with **Not Now**. To never see it, turn off **In-App Ratings & Reviews** in the iPhone Settings › **App Store**.

### Is there a website?
Yes, this one: the complete documentation of the app. Open it from **Settings › Website**.

## Your data

### Can I set a goal, like 3 workouts a week?
Yes, in **Me › Goals**: tap **+**, then choose workouts, training time, exercises or calories, per week, month or year, or between two dates (**Custom**). The progress bar fills with your completed workouts, and the goal turns indigo as soon as it's reached. See [Goals](history-and-statistics.html#goals-your-targets).

### Are my workouts saved in Apple Health?
Yes, if you turn on **Settings › Permissions › Apple Health**. Every completed workout is added to the Health app, and the workouts you delete from the history are deleted there too. See [Apple Health](history-and-statistics.html#apple-health).

### Where are my workouts stored?
Only on your device. The developer has no access to them. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

### Are my workouts synced with iCloud or between my devices?
No. To copy workouts to another device, [export them](import-export.html#move-your-library-to-a-new-device) and import them there (Pro).

### I'm changing phones. How do I keep my workouts?
Before switching, export everything: at the root of My Workouts, **⋯ › Select › Select All › ⋯ › Export Data**, and save the file in iCloud Drive. On the new phone, **⋯ › Import Data**. An iPhone backup restored on the new phone (iCloud or computer) also brings back the app's data.

### What happens if I delete the app?
Your workouts, folders, history, settings and unused AI workout purchases are deleted from the device. Export your workouts first if you want to keep them. Your Pro subscription is not affected: it's linked to your Apple Account.

### Can I export my history?
No. Exports contain folders and workouts only.

### How do I delete my history?
**Settings › Data › Delete Workout History**. It deletes the whole history (not your workouts). To delete a single entry, touch and hold it under the calendar of **Me › Workouts**, then **Delete Entry**.

### I deleted a workout by mistake. Can I get it back?
No, deleting can't be undone. If you had exported it, import the file again.

## Workouts

### Why can't I edit a workout?
Editing the blocks of a workout is a [Pro](pro.html) feature. Without Pro you can rename, duplicate, move and delete workouts, and create new ones from templates or with AI.

### Why can't I save my workout?
The **Create** / **Save** button stays grey while the name is empty or already used by another workout of the same folder. Exercises need a name and a duration above 0; rests need a duration above 0.

### Why does it say “Name Already Used”?
Two workouts (or two folders) of the same folder can't have the same name, whatever the upper/lower case. Choose another name, or move one of them to another folder.

### How do I make intervals or circuits?
Use a **group**: put the exercises and rests of one round inside, and set the number of rounds. See [Groups](creating-workouts.html#groups).

### Can I reorder blocks?
Yes: touch and hold a block › **Move › Move Up / Move Down**, or move it **Into a Group** or **Out of** its group.

### Can I set repetitions instead of a duration?
Every block is timed, because the app is a timer. For reps, use an exercise long enough to do them (for example 60 s), or the **EMOM** or **AMRAP** templates.

### Can I share a workout with a friend?
Yes, with Pro: export it, send the file, and your friend imports it (they need Pro to import). See [Import & Export](import-export.html).

## The timer

### Why don't I hear the beeps?
- Check **Settings › Sounds** in the app.
- Turn up the **media volume** with the volume buttons during the workout (the ringer volume doesn't matter, and silent mode doesn't block the beeps).
- If you use Bluetooth headphones or a speaker, the sound goes there.

### Does the timer keep running when I lock my phone?
Yes. The time stays exact, the beeps keep playing and the [Live Activity](widgets.html#live-activity) shows the countdown on the Lock Screen.

### Why isn't my workout in the history?
Only workouts that reach the end are saved. If you closed the timer with **✕ › End**, it isn't recorded. Tip: to finish quickly, tap **⏩** until the end — the workout then counts as completed.

### Why is the “Time” longer than the planned duration?
**Time** is the real time from start to finish, **pauses included**, plus the 10-second **Get Ready**.

### What does ⏪ do?
It restarts the current step. If the step started less than 2 seconds ago, it goes back to the previous step.

### Can I change the 10-second Get Ready?
No, it's always 10 seconds.

### Can I turn the voice off?
Yes: **Settings › Voice Coach**. The beeps stay on. To make the timer completely silent, turn off **Settings › Sounds**.

## Apple Watch, heart rate and calories

### Is there an Apple Watch app?
Yes, with [Workout Planner Pro](pro.html). Play your workouts on the Watch, or start them on the iPhone with the Watch measuring your heart rate and calories. See [Apple Watch](apple-watch.html).

### Why doesn't my workout have a heart rate?
The heart rate is measured only by the Apple Watch app (Pro): play the workout on the Watch, or start it on the iPhone with the Watch on your wrist, and allow the heart rate the first time.

### Why doesn't my workout have calories?
Calories are calculated from the heart rate, at the end of the workout, **only if Me › Profile is completely filled in** (name, sex, birthday, height and weight). See [Heart rate and calories](history-and-statistics.html#heart-rate-and-calories).

### Why are the calories different in the Health app?
The Health app shows the calories calculated by Apple, its own way. Workout Planner calculates them from your heart rate and your profile.

## AI workouts

### Why does it say “Apple Intelligence Needed”?
Your device doesn't support Apple Intelligence, it's turned off, or it's still downloading. See [Requirements](ai-workouts.html#requirements).

### Was I charged for a workout that failed?
No. A purchase is only used once a workout has been created. Go back to **⋯ › Create a Workout › With AI** and try again: you won't be charged a second time.

### Apple Intelligence declined my request. Why?
Some combinations of answers or some words in the notes are refused by Apple's model or its safety filter. Change the goal or the training style, or rephrase or remove your notes. See [When something goes wrong](ai-workouts.html#when-something-goes-wrong).

### I have Pro. When do I get my free AI workout?
Every calendar month, from the 1st. It doesn't carry over to the next month.

### The workout doesn't fit me. Can I change it?
With Pro, edit it like any other workout. You can also create a new one with more precise notes.

## Pro and purchases

### How do I cancel my subscription?
**Settings › Manage Subscription** in the app, or the Settings app › **[your name]** › **Subscriptions** › **Workout Planner Pro** › **Cancel Subscription**. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

### I paid for Pro but the app doesn't show it.
Tap **Settings › Restore Purchases** and check that you are signed in with the same Apple Account as for the purchase.

### Can I use Pro on my iPhone and my iPad?
Yes, with the same Apple Account. Use **Restore Purchases** on the second device if needed.

### Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### Is there a free trial?
Yes: the first time you subscribe, Pro Yearly and Pro Monthly start with **7 days free** (once per Apple Account). Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the trial and nothing is charged. See [Free trial](pro.html#7-day-free-trial).

### Is Pro shared with my family?
No, Family Sharing isn't available for Workout Planner Pro.

## Widgets and notifications

### The widget says “No workouts yet”.
Create a workout in the app (with a duration above 0), then open and leave the app once to refresh the widget.

### The widget shows old information.
Open Workout Planner once: widgets are refreshed when you open and leave the app and after each workout.

### I don't receive the reminders.
Check **Settings › Notifications** in the app, the iOS Settings app › **Notifications** › **Workout Planner**, and your Focus modes. See [Notifications](notifications.html).

### Can I change the time of the reminders?
No, they are at 7:00 and 22:00 (and at 7:15 for the goal reminders). You can turn them off in **Settings › Notifications**.
