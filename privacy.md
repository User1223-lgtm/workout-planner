# Privacy Policy — Workout Planner

Effective date: November 18, 2026 · Last updated: November 24, 2026

Workout Planner (“the app”) is developed by Thomas Servant-Plagne. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## In short

Workout Planner does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Data stored on your device

Your workouts, folders, workout history (including heart rate and calories), statistics, goals, profile and settings are stored only on your device. The Home Screen widgets, the Live Activity and the Apple Watch app read this data on your devices. The developer has no access to it.

## Profile

**Me › Profile** (name, sex, birthday, height and weight) is optional. It is stored only on your device and sent only to your own Apple Watch. It is used on your devices to say your first name in the reminders and the voice coach, and to calculate the calories of your workouts. It is never sent to the developer or to any server.

You stay in control of this data:

- You can delete workouts and folders at any time in the app.
- **Settings › Delete Workout History** deletes your whole workout history and statistics.
- **Settings › Delete Profile** empties your profile.
- Deleting the app deletes all its data from your device.

If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, this data can be included in that backup, which is managed by Apple and by you, not by the developer.

## Apple Health and Apple Watch

If you turn on **Settings › Apple Health**, the app saves your completed workouts (start, end and name of the workout) in Apple’s Health app, on your device. To keep Health in step with your history, the app reads only the workouts saved by Workout Planner, and deletes those you delete from your history.

With the Apple Watch app (Workout Planner Pro), the Watch reads your **heart rate** during a workout, with your permission. The heart rate and the calories calculated from it are kept in your workout history, on your devices, and saved in the Health app when the option above is on. The app doesn’t read any other health data.

The iPhone and the Apple Watch exchange your workouts, goals, history and profile directly with each other (Apple’s WatchConnectivity), never through a server.

Health data is never sent to the developer or to any server, and is never used for advertising or shared with third parties. You can turn the option off at any time, and manage or delete this data in the Health app.

## AI workouts

Workouts created “With AI” are generated on your device by Apple Intelligence. Your answers to the questionnaire are not sent to the developer or to any server.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment information. Apple’s privacy policy applies to these transactions: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Notifications

The daily reminders, the goal reminders and the monthly reminder for Pro subscribers are local notifications scheduled on your device. No data is sent to a server.

## Voice coach

The voice of the timer is generated on your device by iOS and watchOS. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

## Sharing

When you share your statistics or a workout, or export a workout, the app creates an image or a file and opens the share sheet. You choose where it goes.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
