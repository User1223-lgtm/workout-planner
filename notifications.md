# Notifications

<p class="lead">Two gentle reminders a day, reminders for your goals, and one a month for Pro subscribers. All of them are created on your device.</p>

## The reminders

| When | Title | Message | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| Every day at **7:00** | *A beautiful day awaits you!* | *A workout today?* | Everyone |
| Every day at **22:00** | *This beautiful day is coming to an end!* | *Rest well!* | Everyone |
| The **1st of every month at 0:00** | *Your free AI workout is here!* | *With Workout Planner Pro, you get 1 free AI workout this month. Create it now!* | [Pro](pro.html) subscribers |

When your first name is filled in [Me › Profile](history-and-statistics.html#profile), these three titles say it: *A beautiful day awaits you, Thomas!*, *This beautiful day is coming to an end, Thomas!*, *Your free AI workout is here, Thomas!*

Times follow the clock of your device (local time). The reminders are **local notifications**: they are scheduled by the app on your device, and nothing is sent to a server.

The notifications are shown **even while you are using Workout Planner**, as a banner with a sound, like in any other app.

## Goal reminders

Each [goal](history-and-statistics.html#goals-your-targets) of Me › Goals sends up to four reminders per period. The title is the goal and its dates, for example *3 workouts · 23/11/2026 – 29/11/2026*; the message gives the time left and the progress:

| When | Message |
|---|---|
| **7:15** on the day half of the period is reached | *4 days left · 1 / 3 so far* |
| **7:15** on the day 90% of the period is reached | *2 days left · 1 / 3 so far* |
| **7:15** on the last day | *Last day · 1 / 3 so far* |
| **0:00** when the period ends | *Time's up · Completed (3 / 3)* or *Time's up · Failed (1 / 3)* |

- The three morning reminders stop as soon as the goal is reached. The one at the end is always sent.
- Two reminders never come the same day: for a weekly goal, the 90% day is already the last day, so there is one reminder that morning (3 in all).
- They are updated after each completed workout and each goal added or deleted, so the progress they show is always right. Weekly, monthly and yearly goals get the reminders of the current period and of the next one.

## The monthly pop-up for Pro

Once a month, when a Pro subscriber opens the app and hasn't used the free AI workout of the month yet, a pop-up appears:

> **Your Free AI Workout**
> With Workout Planner Pro, you get 1 free AI workout this month. It doesn't carry over to next month.
>
> **Create It** · **Later**

**Create It** opens the AI questionnaire directly (the workout is saved at the root of My Workouts). **Later** closes the pop-up; it won't show again this month. The pop-up never interrupts a workout or the Pro screen. See [AI Workouts](ai-workouts.html#price-and-access).

## Turn notifications on or off

- **In the app**: **Settings › Notifications**. When it is off, all the reminders above (daily, goals and Pro) are removed. Turn it back on to schedule them again.
- **The first time**, iOS asks whether Workout Planner may send notifications. If you choose **Don't Allow**, the **Notifications** switch in the app is turned off and greyed out, with the message *Notifications are not allowed for Workout Planner. You can allow them in the iOS Settings app.*
- **To allow them later**: open the iOS **Settings** app › **Notifications** › **Workout Planner** › **Allow Notifications**, then come back to the app.

<blockquote class="tip" markdown="1">

Don't receive the reminders? Check that **Settings › Notifications** is on in the app, that notifications are allowed in the iOS Settings app, and that a **Focus** (Sleep, Do Not Disturb…) isn't hiding them.

</blockquote>

The monthly reminder is added when your Pro subscription starts, and removed when it ends.
