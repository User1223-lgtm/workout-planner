## Get the app

{% include screen.html image="watch-pro.jpg" alt="The Apple Watch without Pro" caption="Without Pro" class="right watch" %}

The Apple Watch app comes with Workout Planner: if your Apple Watch is paired with your iPhone, it installs by itself (or open the **Watch** app on your iPhone, find **Workout Planner** and tap **Install**).

- The Watch app is part of **[Workout Planner Pro](pro.html)**. Pro is bought only in the app on your **iPhone or iPad**: without Pro, the Watch shows *The Apple Watch app is part of Workout Planner Pro* and asks you to subscribe there, then to open the app on the Watch again.
- Everything comes from your iPhone: open Workout Planner on your iPhone once, and your workouts appear on the Watch. They stay there, even when the iPhone isn't nearby, and are updated each time you add, edit or delete a workout on the iPhone.
- The first time you start a workout on the Watch, it asks to read your **heart rate** (and to save workouts in Health). Allow it to see your heart rate.

## Your workouts

  {% include screen.html image="watch-workout.jpg" alt="The page of a workout on the Apple Watch" caption="A workout" class="watch" %}

The Watch shows **My Workouts** like your iPhone: your folders first, then your workouts, in alphabetical order. Tap a folder to open its content. Goals, the history and the statistics stay on your iPhone, and workouts are created and edited only on the iPhone: with no workouts yet, the Watch says *Manage your workouts in the app on your iPhone*.

Tap a workout to open its page, like on the iPhone: its description, its **duration**, its number of **exercises** and **blocks**, the **Start Workout** button and the **Program**, where you can open each group to see what's inside.

## Play a workout on the Watch

Tap **Start Workout**, then confirm with **Start**. The timer starts with the 10-second **Get Ready**, then plays every step, like on the iPhone. The iPhone doesn't need to be nearby.

Swipe up and down to switch between three pages:

<div class="screens">
  {% include screen.html image="watch-timer.jpg" alt="The timer on the Apple Watch, in the first of ten rounds" caption="Timer" class="watch" %}
  {% include screen.html image="watch-notes.jpg" alt="The notes of the step and the next step" caption="Notes" class="watch" %}
  {% include screen.html image="watch-controls.jpg" alt="Previous, Pause, Next and End Workout" caption="Controls" class="watch" %}
</div>

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Timer** | The progress of the whole workout, the rounds you're in (every group, from the outermost to the innermost, like *↻ Tabata 3/8*), the kind of step (*Exercise*, *Rest*, *Get Ready*), a large ring with the name of the step and its countdown, and your **heart rate** live at the bottom. Nothing moves when you pause: *Paused* appears in the ring. |
| **Notes** | The description of the current step, and the **next** step. |
| **Controls** | **Previous**, **Pause / Resume**, **Next** and **End Workout**. |

- **Haptics** replace the beeps: a tap when an exercise starts, another when a rest starts, one **halfway** through long steps, one per second for the last **3-2-1**, and a success tap at the end.
- The [voice coach](timer.html#voice-coach) speaks on the Watch speaker or on your AirPods, when **Sounds** and **Voice Coach** are on in the iPhone settings.
- **End Workout** asks for a confirmation, with **End** and **Cancel**. A workout ended early isn't saved.

At the end, the Watch shows **Workout Complete** with the time, the exercises, the average heart rate and the calories. The workout goes into the history of your iPhone (right away, or as soon as the iPhone is nearby), with its heart rate chart, and into the Health app when [Apple Health](history-and-statistics.html#apple-health) is on.

## Smart Stack

{% include screen.html image="watch-smart-stack.jpg" alt="The Workout in Progress card in the Smart Stack" caption="The Smart Stack during a workout" class="right watch" %}

During a workout played on the Watch, the **Smart Stack** (turn the Digital Crown from the watch face) shows a **Workout in Progress** card: the kind of step, its name, its countdown, and the workout with the number of the step (*Boxing Rounds · 3/23*). Tap it to go back to the timer. It disappears at the end of the workout.

Live Activities are for workouts played on the iPhone: they also appear on the Watch, like the notifications of your iPhone.

## One workout at a time

<div class="screens">
  {% include screen.html image="iphone-watch-lock.jpg" alt="The iPhone during a workout on the Apple Watch" caption="Started on the Watch" %}
  {% include screen.html image="watch-locked.jpg" alt="The Apple Watch during a workout on the iPhone" caption="Started on the iPhone" class="watch" %}
</div>

A workout always runs on the device where you started it, and the other one is **locked** until it ends:

- **Started on the Watch**: your iPhone shows *Workout on Apple Watch*.
- **Started on the iPhone**: the Watch shows *Workout on your iPhone or iPad*.

Nothing else can be used, and no other workout can start, until the workout ends on the device playing it. If that device stops answering (app closed, empty battery), the other one unlocks by itself a few minutes after the expected end.

## Heart rate and calories

The heart rate is measured only during a workout played on the Watch. The calories are **calculated from this heart rate and from your profile**, at the end of the workout, and only if **Me › Profile** is completely filled in. See [Heart rate and calories](history-and-statistics.html#heart-rate-and-calories).

## Watch face complication

Add Workout Planner to your watch face: touch and hold the watch face, tap **Edit**, swipe to the complications, choose a place, then **Workout Planner**. It opens the app, to start a workout in a few taps.

## Good to know

- The Watch app needs watchOS 26.5 or later.
- The Watch can't create or edit workouts: do it on your iPhone, and the Watch gets them right away.
- Goals, the history and the statistics are on your iPhone.
