## Get the app

The Apple Watch app comes with Workout Planner: if your Apple Watch is paired with your iPhone, it installs by itself (or open the **Watch** app on your iPhone, find **Workout Planner** and tap **Install**).

- The Watch app is part of **[Workout Planner Pro](pro.html)**. Without Pro, it shows the Pro screen: subscribe right on the Watch (**Pro Yearly** or **Pro Monthly**, with the [7-day free trial](pro.html#7-day-free-trial) if you can still get it, confirmed by double-clicking the side button), tap **Restore Purchases**, or subscribe in the app on your iPhone. Pro is linked to your Apple Account: bought on the Watch, it's also active on your iPhone, and the other way around.
- Everything comes from your iPhone: open Workout Planner on your iPhone once, and your workouts appear on the Watch. They stay there, even when the iPhone isn't nearby, and are updated each time something changes on the iPhone.
- The first time you start a workout on the Watch, it asks to read your **heart rate** (and to save workouts in Health). Allow it to see your heart rate and calories.

## Your workouts

The Watch opens on **Workouts**: your workouts, in alphabetical order, with their folder and duration. Tap one to start it.

## Play a workout on the Watch

Tap a workout in **Workouts**: the timer starts with the 10-second **Get Ready**, then plays every step, like on the iPhone. The iPhone doesn't need to be nearby.

Swipe up and down to switch between three pages, like in Apple's Workout app:

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Timer** | The kind of step (*EXERCISE*, *REST*…), its name, the round, the ring and the countdown, the time left for the whole workout, your **heart rate** ♥ and your **calories**. Tap the ring to pause or resume. |
| **Controls** | **Previous**, **Pause / Resume**, **Next** and **End**. After a tap, the Watch goes back to the timer. |
| **Music** | Controls what's playing on the Watch or the iPhone. |

- **Haptics** replace the beeps: a tap when an exercise starts, another when a rest starts, one **halfway** through each step of 10 seconds or more, one per second for the last **3-2-1**, and a success tap at the end.
- The [voice coach](timer.html#voice-coach) speaks on the Watch speaker or on your AirPods, when **Sounds** and **Voice Coach** are on in the iPhone settings.
- With your wrist down, the screen stays on the timer (Always On), dimmed to save battery.
- **End** asks **End Workout?** with **Cancel** and **End**. A workout ended early isn't saved.

At the end, the Watch shows **Workout Complete** with the time, the exercises, the average heart rate and the calories. The workout goes into the history of your iPhone (right away, or as soon as the iPhone is nearby), with its heart rate chart, and into the Health app when [Apple Health](history-and-statistics.html#apple-health) is on.

## One workout at a time

A workout runs on one device at a time, and the other one is **locked** until it ends.

Both devices show the same lock screen: the device playing the workout (*Workout on Apple Watch* or *Workout on iPhone*), the name of the workout, the current step and its countdown, *Step 3 of 12* (or *Paused*), and *Finish or end it on your … to use the app*. Nothing else can be used, and no other workout can start, until the workout ends.

- **Started on the Watch** (from your list of workouts, the only way to start one on the Watch): your iPhone is locked.
- **Started on the iPhone** with your Apple Watch on your wrist: Workout Planner opens by itself on the Watch, which is locked. Pause, skip or end the workout on the iPhone. Meanwhile, the Watch measures your **heart rate**: the iPhone shows it live under the progress bar, with the calories, and saves them with the workout. The Watch taps at each step change; the voice coach speaks on the iPhone.
- If the device playing the workout stops answering (app closed, empty battery), the other one unlocks by itself a few minutes after the expected end.

## Heart rate and calories

The heart rate is measured only by the Apple Watch, during a workout played on the Watch, or played on the iPhone with the Watch on your wrist. The calories are **calculated from this heart rate and from your profile**, at the end of the workout, and only if **Me › Profile** is completely filled in. See [Heart rate and calories](history-and-statistics.html#heart-rate-and-calories).

## Watch face complication

Add Workout Planner to your watch face: touch and hold the watch face, tap **Edit**, swipe to the complications, choose a place, then **Workout Planner**. It opens the app, to start a workout in one tap. It's also available in the **Smart Stack** (turn the Digital Crown from the watch face).

## Good to know

- The Watch app needs watchOS 26.5 or later.
- The Watch can't create or edit workouts: do it on your iPhone, and the Watch gets them right away.
- Goals, the history and the statistics are on your iPhone.
