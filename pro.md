## What Pro unlocks

{% include screen.html image="pro.jpg" alt="The Workout Planner Pro screen" caption="The Pro screen" class="right" %}

| Pro feature | Where | Without Pro |
|---|---|---|
| **Create workouts from scratch** | ⋯ › Create a Workout › From Scratch | Use templates or AI |
| **Edit any workout** | Edit Workout (workout page or touch and hold) | You can still rename, duplicate, move and delete |
| **Import workouts and folders** | ⋯ › Import Data | – |
| **Export workouts and folders** | Export Workout / Export Folder / Export Data | – |
| **1 free AI workout every month** | ⋯ › Create a Workout › With AI | Each AI workout is bought separately |
| **Apple Watch app**, with heart rate and calories | Your Apple Watch | The Watch app shows the Pro screen |

Everything else is free: templates, the timer and its voice coach, folders, search, history, statistics, goals, your profile, sharing your stats, widgets, the Live Activity, notifications and Apple Health.

Without Pro, Pro actions are shown with a **gold crown**. Tapping one opens the Pro screen.

## Plans

| Plan | Billing | Price in France |
|---|---|---|
| **Pro Yearly** | Once a year | €14.99/year |
| **Pro Monthly** | Every month | €3.99/month |

Both plans unlock exactly the same features. Prices depend on your country and are always shown in your currency on the Pro screen before you subscribe. There is no free trial.

Subscriptions **renew automatically** until you cancel them. Payment is charged to your Apple Account and handled entirely by Apple.

## Subscribe

1. Open the Pro screen: **Settings › Unlock Workout Planner Pro**, or tap any Pro action with a crown. (The screen is also shown once, at the first launch.) You can also subscribe on your [Apple Watch](apple-watch.html#get-the-app), from the Workout Planner app.
2. Choose **Pro Yearly** or **Pro Monthly**.
3. Tap **Subscribe** and confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your password.

Pro is active immediately. **Settings** then shows **Workout Planner Pro Unlocked**.

The Pro screen also has links to the **Terms of Service** and the **Privacy Policy**, a **Restore Subscription** button, and **✕** to close it without subscribing.

## Manage, change plan or cancel

- **In the app**: **Settings › Manage Subscription** opens Apple's subscription sheet. You can switch between monthly and yearly, or cancel.
- **On your iPhone or iPad**: Settings app › **[your name]** › **Subscriptions** › **Workout Planner Pro**.

When you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

Refunds are handled by Apple, at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Restore purchases

Pro is linked to your **Apple Account**, not to a device. To use it on another iPhone or iPad, or after reinstalling the app:

1. Sign in with the same Apple Account in the App Store.
2. Open Workout Planner: Pro is usually detected automatically.
3. If not, tap **Settings › Restore Purchases** (or **Restore Subscription** on the Pro screen).

The app answers with one of these messages:

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Your Pro subscription has been restored.* | Pro is active. |
| *No active Pro subscription was found for this Apple Account.* | The subscription has ended, or it was bought with another Apple Account. |
| *The purchases could not be restored. Please try again later.* | The App Store couldn't be reached. Check your connection and try again. |

Family Sharing isn't available for Workout Planner Pro.

## When Pro ends

Nothing is deleted. Your workouts, folders, history and AI workouts stay exactly as they are, and you can still start, rename, duplicate, move and delete them. Only the Pro actions are locked again. Unused free AI workouts don't carry over.

## AI workout purchases

AI workouts can be bought one by one, with or without Pro (€1.99 each in France). They are **consumable**: one purchase creates one workout. The free monthly AI workout of Pro is always used first.

- You only pay for a workout that is actually created: if creation fails, the purchase is kept for the next try.
- Unused AI workouts are stored on the device where they were bought. They can't be restored with *Restore Purchases*, so use them before deleting the app.

Details: [AI Workouts › Price and access](ai-workouts.html#price-and-access).
