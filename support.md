# Workout Planner — Support

<p class="lead">Need help? Write to <a href="mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}">{{ site.contact_email }}</a>. Answers usually within 48 hours.</p>

To help us answer quickly, please include your **device model**, its **iOS version**, the **app version** (shown in **Settings › Version**) and, if possible, a screenshot.

## Quick answers

### How do I restore my Pro subscription?
Open **Settings › Restore Purchases**, or the **Restore Subscription** button in the Pro screen. Use the same Apple Account as for the purchase. [More about Pro](pro.html#restore-purchases)

### How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone: Settings › **[your name]** › **Subscriptions** › **Workout Planner Pro** › **Cancel Subscription**. You can also use **Settings › Manage Subscription** in the app. [More](pro.html#manage-change-plan-or-cancel)

### Why can't I create a workout with AI?
AI workouts are created on your device by Apple Intelligence. You need an iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings › **Apple Intelligence & Siri**). Right after you turn it on, the device may need a few minutes to download it. [More](ai-workouts.html#requirements)

### I paid for an AI workout but it wasn't created.
A purchase is only used once a workout has been created. Go back to **⋯ › Create a Workout › With AI** and try again: you won't be charged a second time. [More](ai-workouts.html#when-something-goes-wrong)

### How do I add the widgets?
Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap **Edit › Add Widget**, then search for **Workout Planner**. Touch and hold a widget and tap **Edit Widget** to choose what it shows. [More](widgets.html)

### Are my workouts backed up?
Your workouts are stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them. With Pro, you can export your workouts and folders to a file and import them again later. [More](import-export.html)

### How do I delete my workout history?
**Settings › Data › Delete Workout History**. Your saved workouts are kept. [More](history-and-statistics.html#delete-your-history)

### I don't hear the timer beeps.
Check **Settings › Sounds**, and turn up the media volume during the workout. Silent mode doesn't block the beeps. [More](timer.html#sounds)

### How can I rate Workout Planner?
**Settings › Rate on the App Store** opens the review page of the app. Thank you, it really helps! [More](faq.html#how-can-i-rate-the-app-or-leave-a-review)

### My workout isn't in the history.
Only workouts that reach the end are saved. A workout closed with **✕ › End** isn't recorded. [More](history-and-statistics.html#what-is-recorded)
