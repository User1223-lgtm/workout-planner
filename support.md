# Workout Planner — Support

Need help? Write to [ser.pla.thomas@gmail.com](mailto:ser.pla.thomas@gmail.com). Answers usually within 48 hours.

### How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open Settings > Restore Purchases, or the Restore button in the Pro screen. Use the same Apple Account as for the purchase.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Workout Planner Pro > Cancel Subscription. You can also use Settings > Manage Subscription in the app.

### Why can't I create a workout with AI?

AI workouts are created on your device by Apple Intelligence. You need an iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with M1 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri). Right after you turn it on, the device may need a few minutes to download it.

### I paid for an AI workout but it wasn't created.

A purchase is only used once a workout has been created. Go back to + > Create a Workout > With AI and try again: you won't be charged a second time.

### How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit > Add Widget, then search for Workout Planner. Touch and hold a widget and tap Edit Widget to choose what it shows.

### Are my workouts backed up?

Your workouts are stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them. With Pro, you can export your workouts and folders to a file and import them again later.
